# Onboarding and map-flow verification

The final local end-to-end smoke flow passed with 114 initial pins and 8 zone rings. When a zone is selected, the test observed 14 individual bay pins and confirmed that zone rings are hidden at street-level expansion, then restored after zooming out. It also verified the first-use dialog appears and dismisses correctly, the mobile Map tools chip is available and expands its panel, and the citizen display name `Map Tester` persists into the authenticated dashboard.

The selected pin successfully opens the reservation drawer, including arrival time and accessibility-permit controls. A demonstration hold produces a confirmation receipt. No duplicate Google Maps loader errors or legacy marker warnings were recorded. Visual review confirmed a legible three-step first-use guide with a clear completion action.
